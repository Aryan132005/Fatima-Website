About Fatima

About Fatima

I'm Fatima Abreu, Career Clarity Coach, Consultant, Founder of Care to Voice, and Author of Be the Reason You Thrive. With broad global corporate experience, I've partnered with leaders and organisations across Reward, Workforce, and people strategy during periods of growth, change, and evolving workforce expectations.

Throughout my career, I noticed a recurring pattern: organisations were navigating constant transformation while many professionals — despite appearing successful externally — felt uncertain about their direction, identity, or next step. As AI and changing ways of working continue to reshape careers, that tension is becoming increasingly common.

That is why I founded Care to Voice — a platform focused on career clarity, self-leadership, and navigating change in a changing world. Through consulting, coaching, workshops, and strategic conversations, I support professionals and organisations in building clearer direction, adaptability, and more human-centred approaches to leadership, workforce strategy, and the future of work.

Fatima Y. Abreu Arellano
